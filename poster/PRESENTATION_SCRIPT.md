# Before the Statin: 2-minute poster script

**Noor Al Ahmad · College of Pharmacy, Al Ain University**
Main message: **bergamot as an early, preventive step in young patients with high cholesterol, to delay the need for statins.**
About 280 words, roughly 2 minutes. *Italic lines in brackets are actions, not words to say.* **Bold** = say it slowly.

---

### 1. The hook (0:00–0:20)
*(Look at the audience. Pause after the question.)*

A 16-year-old has high cholesterol. Too high to ignore, but not high enough for a statin.
So what do we do today? **Usually, we just wait.**
*(pause)*
My poster asks: **what if we didn't wait?**

### 2. Why waiting costs years (0:20–0:50)
*(Point to the graph and trace each line)*

Cholesterol damage builds up every year, like water filling a bucket.
*(red dashed line)* When it reaches this line, heart disease can begin.
*(red line)* A young patient with high cholesterol who is left untreated can get there by about **40.**
*(green line)* Lower it early, and they get there about **six years later.**
Genetic studies show that lowering LDL from early life cuts coronary risk by about **55%.**
**Every year we wait is a year of damage.**

### 3. Find them early (0:50–1:05)
*(Point to section 02)*

First, we find the right patients: family history, a cholesterol test, Lp(a) and genetic risk.
Young patients with **monogenic FH** go straight to guideline statin care.
But many others sit in the **grey zone**, and that's where bergamot could help.

### 4. Bergamot: the bridge before the statin (1:05–1:40)
*(Point to the fruit, then the body diagram)*

**Citrus bergamot** is a citrus from southern Italy. Some of its polyphenols may act on the same enzyme as statins.
In adult trials it lowered LDL by about **40 mg/dL**, and also improved triglycerides, sugar, blood pressure and fatty liver.
That's why I see it as a possible **prophylactic bridge**: start it early, lower cholesterol gently, and **delay the day a statin is needed.**
*(point to section 04)* To be honest: those trials were in adults, and **it hasn't been tested in young patients yet.** That's the next step.

### 5. The take-home (1:40–2:00)
*(Count on your fingers)*

So, three points:
**One:** find high cholesterol early in young patients.
**Two:** don't just wait. Act in the grey zone.
**Three:** bergamot could be that first step, but it needs trials in young people.
**Protect the heart before the statin.**
*(Point to the QR code)* All sources are here. Thank you!

## If people ask questions

**"So bergamot replaces statins?"**
No. It's an idea for young patients who **don't yet need** a statin, to delay that point. Anyone who meets statin criteria, especially with monogenic FH, should get the statin.

**"Is there proof it works in young people?"**
Not yet. The evidence is from adult trials, and its certainty is low. That's exactly why trials in young patients are needed before it's recommended.

**"How much does it lower cholesterol compared with a statin?"**
In adult studies, about 40 mg/dL on average. Statins usually lower LDL by 30–50% or more, so bergamot is gentler. That's why it fits before a statin, not instead of one.

**"Is it safe?"**
Adult trials reported it was well tolerated. Safety in young people still needs to be studied.

**"Is the graph real data?"**
It's an example model of a proven idea: the earlier cholesterol is lowered, the later heart disease appears.

### Tips
- **Really pause** after "Usually, we just wait." It's the moment that hooks people.
- **Bring a real bergamot,** or a photo. People remember what they can see.
- **Trace the graph lines with your finger** as you talk.
- Say the honesty line in part 4 confidently. Doctors trust a speaker who knows the limits.
- Practise with a timer. If you run long, shorten part 3.
