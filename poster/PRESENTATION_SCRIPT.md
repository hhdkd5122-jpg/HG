# Before the Statin: 2-minute poster script

**Noor Al Ahmad · College of Pharmacy, Al Ain University**

> **Hypothesis:** If a young patient with high cholesterol starts *Citrus bergamot* now, can we delay the need for a statin in the long run?

About 290 words, roughly 2 minutes. *Italic lines in brackets are actions, not words.* **Bold** = slow down and land it.

---

### 1. The question (0:00–0:20)
*(Look at the audience, not the poster. Speak slowly.)*

Picture a 16-year-old. Healthy. Active. And already carrying cholesterol that's quietly too high.
Not high enough for a statin. So today, we tell them: **"Come back later."**
*(pause)*
I want to ask a different question.
**What if they started Citrus bergamot today… and never needed a statin until much, much later?**

### 2. Time is the real risk factor (0:20–0:50)
*(Point to the graph. Trace each line with your finger.)*

Cholesterol doesn't hurt you once. It hurts you **every single year.**
Think of a bucket filling drop by drop.
*(red dashed line)* When it fills to here, heart disease begins.
*(red line)* Leave a high-risk young patient alone, and they reach it around **40.**
*(green line)* Lower the drip early, even a little, and they reach it about **six years later.**
And this isn't wishful thinking: genetic studies show that lower LDL from early life means about **55% less coronary disease.**
**The earlier you start, the less you need.**

### 3. Why bergamot? (0:50–1:25)
*(Point to the fruit, then the body diagram)*

Meet **Citrus bergamot**, a bitter green citrus from southern Italy.
Some of its compounds may act on **the very same enzyme that statins block.**
In adult trials, it lowered LDL by about **40 mg/dL**.
And it didn't stop there: it also improved **triglycerides, blood sugar, blood pressure, belly fat and fatty liver.**
**One fruit, five risk factors.**
So my hypothesis is simple: a gentle push, started early, could **keep a young patient below the statin line for years.**

### 4. What we still need to prove (1:25–1:45)
*(Point to section 04. Say this with confidence, not apology.)*

Now, a good scientist tells you what's **missing**.
Every bergamot trial so far was in adults, the evidence is still low certainty, and **no one has tested it in young patients.**
Young patients with monogenic FH still need a statin. This idea is for the **grey zone**, the ones we currently send home to wait.

### 5. The close (1:45–2:00)
*(Step toward the audience. Slow down.)*

We already know **when** heart disease starts: **young.**
We already know **what** drives it: **cholesterol over time.**
The question is whether a fruit can buy our patients **years.**
**I believe it's worth finding out.**
*(Point to the QR code)* Every source is right here. Thank you.

## If people ask questions

**"So bergamot replaces statins?"**
No. It's for young patients who don't need a statin *yet*, to delay that point. Anyone who meets statin criteria, especially with monogenic FH, should take the statin.

**"Is there proof it works in young people?"**
Not yet. That's why this is a hypothesis. The adult data are promising, so the next step is a proper trial in young patients.

**"How strong is it compared with a statin?"**
Gentler. About 40 mg/dL in adult studies, while statins usually lower LDL by 30–50% or more. That's exactly why it fits *before* a statin, not instead of one.

**"Is it safe?"**
Adult trials reported it was well tolerated. Safety in young people still has to be studied.

**"Is the graph real data?"**
It's a model of a proven principle: the earlier LDL is lowered, the later heart disease appears.

---

### Delivery tips
- **The pause after "Come back later"** is your most powerful moment. Hold it for two seconds.
- **"One fruit, five risk factors"**: count them on your fingers.
- **Bring a real bergamot,** or a photo. Hold it up when you say "Meet Citrus bergamot."
- **Part 4 is a strength.** Say it calmly; doctors trust a speaker who knows the limits.
- **Your last line is "I believe it's worth finding out."** Say it slowly, then stop. Don't rush to "thank you."
- Practise with a timer. If you run over, trim part 3.
