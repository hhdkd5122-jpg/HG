# Before the Statin: 2-minute poster script

**Noor Al Ahmad · College of Pharmacy, Al Ain University**
About 280 words, roughly 2 minutes at a calm pace. *Italic lines in brackets are what to point at, not what to say.*

---

### 1. Hook (0:00–0:15)
*(Point to the title)*

Good morning. My poster is called **"Before the Statin."**
Heart disease doesn't start at 40. It starts in childhood, silently, years before anyone thinks about a statin.

### 2. The problem (0:15–0:45)
*(Point to the graph in section 01)*

This graph shows cholesterol building up over the years, like water filling a bucket.
When the bucket reaches this red line, heart problems can begin.
A young patient with high genetic risk, the red line, reaches it at about 40.
If we lower their cholesterol early, the green line, they reach it about **six years later**.
Large genetic studies support this: lower LDL from early life gives about **55% lower** coronary risk per mmol/L.

### 3. Detect (0:45–1:05)
*(Point to section 02)*

So first, we need to **find** these young patients early.
We use five simple checks: a polygenic risk score, Lp(a), family history, lipids, and a metabolic profile.
Patients with monogenic FH still go to standard statin care.
The rest, with polygenic or metabolic risk, are the group we focus on.

### 4. Treat early with bergamot (1:05–1:35)
*(Point to the body diagram in section 03)*

For them, we propose **Citrus bergamot**, a Mediterranean citrus rich in polyphenols.
In adult trials, it lowered LDL-C by about **40 mg/dL**, and also improved triglycerides, glucose, blood pressure, waist size and fatty liver.
*(Point to section 04)*
But I want to be honest: the evidence certainty is low, one trial showed no effect, and **no trial has tested bergamot in young patients yet.**

### 5. Our study and close (1:35–2:00)
*(Point to the green "Proposed study" bar)*

That's the gap our proposed trial fills: screen young patients, randomise them to bergamot or placebo for 12 months, and measure LDL-C, glucose, blood pressure and liver fat.
Our goal is simple: **find risk early, act early, and give young patients more healthy years before they ever need a statin.**

## If the judges ask questions

**"Is bergamot replacing statins?"**
No. For monogenic FH or very high LDL-C, statins stay the standard. Bergamot is for young patients who don't yet meet statin criteria, as an early first step.

**"Is the graph real data?"**
It's an illustrative model based on the cumulative-exposure principle from Mendelian randomization studies. Our trial would test it in real patients.

**"Why is the evidence low certainty?"**
The trials are small, short, often used combination products, and were all in adults. That's exactly why a proper trial in young patients is needed.

**"Is bergamot safe?"**
Adult trials reported it was well tolerated. Our trial includes safety checks on liver, muscle and growth.

**"Why genetics?"**
Genetic risk shows who needs help years before cholesterol becomes very high. And Khera (NEJM 2016) showed that high genetic risk is modifiable: a healthy lifestyle cut coronary events by about 46%.

### Tips
- Practise with a timer. If you're over 2 minutes, shorten section 4 first.
- Look at the judges, not the poster. Use your hand to point.
- Say the bold numbers slowly. Those are what people remember.
