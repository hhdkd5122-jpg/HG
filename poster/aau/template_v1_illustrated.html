WHY START EARLY?
Damage adds up: LDL-C harms arteries every year it stays high.
Genes warn early: family history, Lp(a) and genetic scores flag risk decades before symptoms.
Early wins most: lower LDL from early life = ~55% less coronary disease per mmol/L.

Why Citrus Bergamot?

Citrus bergamia is a bitter citrus from Calabria, Italy, rich in flavanones. Two of them, brutieridin and melitidin, carry a statin-like side chain and may act on HMG-CoA reductase, the same enzyme statins block.

STUDY HYPOTHESIS

Starting Citrus bergamot early in young patients with high cholesterol, who do not yet meet statin criteria, will lower LDL-C and linked cardiometabolic risk factors, and delay the need for statin therapy in the long run.

DID YOU KNOW?

Statins came from nature

The first statin, compactin, was isolated from the fungus Penicillium citrinum by Akira Endo in 1976. Lovastatin, from Aspergillus terreus, became the first FDA-approved statin in 1987.

Conclusion

Heart disease begins in youth. Citrus bergamot shows promising multi-target effects in adults, making it a strong candidate to test as an early step that could delay statin therapy in young patients with high cholesterol.

SAFETY NOTE
Citrus bergamot is a dietary supplement, not an approved therapy. Young patients who meet statin criteria, including monogenic FH, should follow guideline care.
