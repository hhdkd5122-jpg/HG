Before the Statin:Citrus Bergamot

Can early Citrus bergamot delay statin therapy in young patients with high cholesterol?
Noor Al Ahmad  |  Department of Pharmacy, Al Ain University, Al Ain, UAE
Background
  • Atherosclerosis starts in youth. Childhood risk factors (ages 3–19) predict adult cardiovascular events in 38,589 people followed for 35 years.
  • Damage is cumulative. Each 1 mmol/L lower LDL-C sustained from early life gives ~55% lower coronary risk, about 3× the benefit of lowering it later.
  • Genes are modifiable risk. High genetic risk with a healthy lifestyle: 46% fewer coronary events.
Jacobs, NEJM 2022 · Ference, JACC 2012 · Khera, NEJM 2016
Why Citrus bergamot?

Citrus bergamia is a citrus grown mainly in Calabria, Italy, and rich in flavanones. Two of them, brutieridin and melitidin, carry a statin-like side chain and are proposed to inhibit HMG-CoA reductase, the statin target.

DID YOU KNOW?
Statins came from nature: compactin was isolated from Penicillium citrinum (Endo, 1976), and lovastatin from Aspergillus terreus became the first FDA-approved statin (1987).
STUDY HYPOTHESIS

Starting Citrus bergamot early in young patients with high cholesterol, who do not yet meet statin criteria, will lower LDL-C and linked cardiometabolic risk factors, and delay the need for statin therapy in the long run.

How bergamot may work
Mechanisms are proposed, mainly from laboratory and small human studies.

Early risk detection
  1. Family historyPremature heart disease in a close relative
  2. Lipid profileLDL-C, triglycerides, HDL-C, apoB
  3. Lipoprotein(a)Genetically determined; measure once
  4. Genetic testingPolygenic risk score; FH variants (LDLR, APOB, PCSK9)
  5. Metabolic profileWaist, blood pressure, glucose, liver fat
Hypothesis pathway
High cholesterol below statin criteria, no monogenic variant: lifestyle + bergamot, re-check every 6–12 months.
Guideline care
Monogenic FH or very high LDL-C: statin as indicated. Bergamot is not a substitute.
Universal lipid screening at 9–11 and 17–21 years (NHLBI 2011); Lp(a) once (EAS 2022).

How to test the hypothesis
CONCLUSION

Atherosclerosis begins in youth and LDL-C damage accumulates over time. Citrus bergamot improves several cardiometabolic risk factors in adults, which makes it a strong candidate to test as an early step that could delay statin therapy in young patients with high cholesterol.

SAFETY NOTE
Citrus bergamot is a dietary supplement, not an approved therapy. Young patients who meet statin criteria, including monogenic FH, should follow guideline care.
